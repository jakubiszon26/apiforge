# UX-41: The selected step no longer looks like an error

- Size: S · Owner review: 15 min · Risk: `visible UI`
- State: Analysis (issue #519) · mock-up posted, **decision needed** (question 1) ·
  for 0.2.1 · found by the owner on the v0.2.0-rc.1 packages, 2026-11-21
- Analysis model: Fable 5.2 · Implementation model: —

## Goal

"I don't like the red outline after clicking a block after all." A
selected step is told apart at a glance, and its mark is not mistaken for
the mark of a failure.

## What is there today, and where it came from

LAY-10 (#294, merged 2026-11-16) replaced a 1.7 px accent border with what
its ticket called "a strong accent frame and handles": a 2.5 px frame in
the accent (224 · 89 · 39) standing 3 px off the card, and four square
corner handles. The owner approved it as a direction on the LAY-00 round 3
and 4 mock-ups (Q9 = A, 2026-11-05). Three things were not seen then:

- the accent and the failure red (196 · 64 · 48) are neighbours: on a
  failed step the red band and border sit inside the orange frame and
  read as one thick red outline;
- the selected step's wires take the accent too, so the whole
  neighbourhood turns orange-red;
- the handles promise a resize a card does not have.

## What the owner will see

- Changed: the frame of a selected card, and the colour of the selected
  step's wires, which follow the frame. Options A, B, C below.
- Removed or hidden: the four corner handles (all options). They do
  nothing today.
- Unchanged: the halo of related cards (teal), the check colour, the
  dimming, Esc; the red band and border of a failure; the accent of the
  buttons and tabs.

## Acceptance criteria

Tests in `crates/apiforge-desktop/src/folder/acceptance_tests/ux41.rs`.

1. A selected card draws the frame of the chosen option and no corner
   handle, at Overview, Compact and Full. `ux41_criterion_1_frame_without_handles`.
2. The selection's colour is one constant, `theme::SELECTION`; the frame
   and the selected step's wires use it, and it differs from
   `theme::DANGER`, `theme::WARNING`, `theme::SUCCESS`, the related
   teal and the check colour. `ux41_criterion_2_one_colour_not_a_status`.
3. On a failed and selected step both the frame and the red signal border
   are drawn. `ux41_criterion_3_failure_shows_under_selection`.
4. LAY-10's criteria hold with "handles" read as "frame": its tests ask
   whether the selection is drawn, not `debug_handles`.
5. Design review: `review_lay10` and a new `review_ux41` at 960 × 540
   and 1600 × 1000, compared with the approved capture.

## Not in scope

The chips of a far value (they keep the variable colour), the accent of
buttons, tabs and the code editor, dark mode (WS-03 #368).
