## Goal

One left sidebar (Files / Library) in every mode, a board that fits a
540 px window with its panels open, and an *Add step* popup that fits it
too. The squeeze of Split at 1600 × 1000 (#161 part 4) is answered by
LAY-16's compact cards, not by this mock-up (decision 2). Source issues: UX-06 #146 (owner
wish), #161 part 4 and #157 (automated findings).
